# Term-ownership map (authoritative for the terms listed)

Rule (author decision 2026-10-21): the OWNER chapter bold-defines the term at its teaching point and owns its glossary row. Every other chapter leaves the term unbolded and, only if the sentence needs it, adds a brief plain parenthetical gloss (optionally "see the *Owner* chapter"). Terms not listed here: follow your plan, applying the same owner rule.

Reassigned by the orchestrator (these override what individual plans assumed): absolute risk reduction → Evidence vs Hype; relative risk reduction → Evidence vs Hype; composite endpoint → Evidence vs Hype; hard endpoint → Evidence vs Hype; surrogate endpoint → Evidence vs Hype; osteoclast → Bone & Joint Health; osteoporosis → Bone & Joint Health; peak bone mass → Childhood & Adolescence; hba1c → Type 2 Diabetes; hypertension → Cardiovascular Disease & Hypertension; hyperkalaemia → Chronic Kidney Disease; insulin resistance → Obesity & Metabolic Syndrome; metabolic syndrome → Obesity & Metabolic Syndrome; weight-inclusive → Obesity & Metabolic Syndrome. Also: SGLT1, brush border, bile salts, micelles, chylomicrons, lipase, pepsin → Digestion & Absorption (Macronutrients names them unbolded); ultra-processed foods → Appetite & Weight Regulation; eGFR → Chronic Kidney Disease; undernutrition, malnutrition → Undernutrition & Malnutrition; sarcopenia, frailty, dysphagia, texture-modified food → Healthy Ageing; confounding, reverse causation, RCT, prospective cohort → Evidence vs Hype (if Evidence vs Hype does not use "confounding", Brain Health & Dementia owns it); anaemia → Micronutrients, Hydration & Electrolytes; coeliac disease → Digestion & Absorption.
