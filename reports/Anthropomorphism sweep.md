# Anthropomorphism sweep (2026-10-22)

**What it is.** Anthropomorphism (the APA style manual's term) gives a human mental act, intention, emotion or moral quality to something that cannot have one: "'just try harder' **misreads** the biology", "the label **says nothing**", "lactation **asks** only a little more", "the gut **pours out** fluid but SGLT1 **keeps working**". Standard scientific usage is not flagged: "the study found", "data show", "guidelines recommend", receptor and signalling language, "insulin resistance", "the reward system".

**Found:** 232 items, 39 major (a reader would notice it) and 193 minor (mild or borderline). Nothing has been changed.

## Decisions for the author

1. **"Defended range"** (appetite and energy-balance chapters, glossary, quiz, appetite island; section ids `#sec-defended`). It is an established research term ("defended level of body fat") but is itself a personification. Keep as a defined technical term, or rename to "regulated range" in text (ids unchanged).
2. **"Water-loving / water-hating / fat-loving"** glosses for hydrophilic/hydrophobic (macronutrients, digestion, the bile structure island). Common textbook glosses; the proposal is "water-attracting / water-repelling". "Fat-loving" is also slightly inaccurate as a gloss for hydrophobic.
3. **"Preferred fuel"** for butyrate and colonocytes (SCFA island, 4×). Standard biochemistry wording; proposal "main fuel".
