# Where a photo or diagram would help (2026-10-22)

From the image sweep: 80 places across 26 chapters where seeing something teaches what the prose cannot. **Photo** = a real photograph or clinical/imaging image; **Diagram** = a drawn, labelled illustration (cells, organs, mechanisms). Priority: ★ high. Each row names the section and the sentence it would sit beside. Candidate free images for most rows are in the review gallery (https://claude.ai/artifact/VDRYzb1KvMVNM7hwkuGXEM).

## Notes

- The waist-measurement image fits several chapters (energy balance, obesity, assessment, menopause); energy balance owns measurement (`#sec-measuring`), so one image there with cross-references avoids repeats.
